# Asset Catalog

The catalog is the master inventory. Priorities apply to the agreed MVP unless a source conflict is called out. Status is **planning readiness**, not asset completion.

**Source shorthand:** `GD##` refers to `documents/game_design/elements/##_*`; `AD##` refers to `documents/architecture/decisions/##_*`. `current main scene` refers to `res://scenes/levels/main_game.tscn` observed in the live editor.
