## Character Scale

All characters use the same base proportions:
- Height: ~1.7m (visitors), ~1.75m (staff)
- Width: 0.5m (capsule collision)
- Characters are ~42% of floor height (4m)

## MVP Approach

For MVP, characters are simple low-poly humanoid shapes or even capsules with minimal detail. The focus is on readable movement and behavior, not individual identity.
