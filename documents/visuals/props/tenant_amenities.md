# Tenant Frontages, Operations & Amenities

## Identity

**Asset IDs:** `3d_tenant_frontage_family`, `3d_prop_operations_room`, `3d_prop_bin`, `3d_prop_garbage`, `3d_prop_bathroom`, `3d_prop_green_space`, `3d_prop_water_feature`, `3d_prop_art_installation`, `3d_prop_seasonal_event_set`  
**Category:** 3D props and reusable prop families  
**Family:** Commercial occupancy, operations, hygiene, and prestige amenities

## Purpose

These assets make spaces legible without turning Janus into individual-shop micromanagement. They communicate tenant presence and lifecycle, staff infrastructure, hygiene demand, and prestige-improving amenity choices.

## Gameplay Context

- Parcels are generated inside player-created zones. Each has frontage to transit/corridors, an automatic entrance, a tenant lifecycle, and a visible name/subtype label.
- Operations Rooms are player-placed 2×2 facilities that spawn staff and define coverage.
- Bins reduce garbage on their floor; garbage is visible only on corridor tiles and triggers cleaners.
- Green spaces, water features, art, and terraces are player-facing prestige/experience choices. Bathrooms answer a concrete visitor need and can become dirty.

## Dependencies & Reuse

- Tenant labels, status icons, and construction overlays depend on [game_ui.md](../ui/game_ui.md) and [overlays.md](../vfx/overlays.md).
- Green-space, water, and art assets must occupy valid decoration/amenity tiles and remain compatible with terrace edges.
- Operations Rooms, bathrooms, and amenities are distinct functional tile elements; their visual footprints must never imply illegal stacking.
- Use a shared architectural palette and material family from [material_system.md](../materials/material_system.md).

## Visual Requirements

- Tenant variety must come from zone-function cues, silhouette, modest awnings/glazing/signage regions, and color blocking—not real-world branding or dense small text.
- Japanese urban identity may appear through compact storefront rhythm, awnings, vertical sign space, vending/utility-like service detail, and restaurant-lantern-like accents, used selectively.
- Do not let signage colors imitate the five zone-overlay colors so closely that placement feedback becomes ambiguous.
- Amenities should create clear focal points and softened public space, not clutter every walkable tile.
- Event/holiday art should be additive, temporary, and subordinate to building readability.

## Scale & Technical Requirements

- Operations Rooms are exactly 2×2 tiles; bins occupy one tile; garbage stays a corridor-tile-level indicator.
- Tenant parcels are variable rectangles generated by the zone system; frontage assets must accommodate frontage segments rather than fixed shop widths.
- No player-facing text may be baked into frontage or amenity art; use localized UI/world-label regions.
- Bathroom footprint, capacity, and unlock are unknown; do not final-produce a fixed module yet.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Players can distinguish a vacant/construction/open/closing tenant parcel from normal floor space.
- A 2×2 Operations Room is unambiguously different from a rentable tenant parcel.
- Bins and litter are visible at normal zoom but do not block path or visual analysis.
- Each prestige amenity is recognizable from silhouette and offers a distinct public-space character.
- A dirty bathroom state would be visible enough to explain visitor avoidance and cleaner demand.
